Journear Passport demo

Testnet demo: Stellar, EVM and NEAR test networks only, nothing here holds real funds.

Log in with email (we send a code to your inbox; Google sign-in works for listed testers) to get a Stellar, an EVM and a NEAR wallet, with no extension and no seed phrase. On NEAR you can write to a guestbook right away with zero NEAR: Journear pays the fee. This page is a partner app built on @hana-id/web. Your keys and your sign-in stay inside Journear Passport’s own windows; this page sees only your addresses and the signatures you approve, and every signature needs your approval in a Journear window.
