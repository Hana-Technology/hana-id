Hana ID demo

Testnet demo: Stellar, EVM and NEAR test networks only, nothing here holds real funds.

Log in with email (a code sent to your inbox; Google sign-in works for listed testers) to get a Stellar, an EVM and a NEAR wallet, with no extension or seed phrase. On NEAR you can write to a guestbook contract right away with zero NEAR: Hana’s relayer pays the fee. This page is a partner app built on @hana-id/web: your keys and your Google sign-in stay inside Hana’s own windows and frame, and this page only sees your addresses and the signatures you approve. Every signature needs your approval in a Hana window.
